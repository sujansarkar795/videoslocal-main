Reading room

Notes on keeping what you are allowed to keep

Three short pieces: one on routine, one on rights, one on the machinery. They are written for people archiving their own work or footage they have licensed — not for filling a hard drive with other people's catalogs.

  • A backup routine for the videos you own Accounts get locked, hacked and deleted. A quarterly export that survives all three, without a subscription. 9 min
  • Fair use, licensing, and the line between backup and republishing Two separate rulebooks apply to every download. Knowing which one you are crossing changes what is safe. 7 min
  • Why a download fails, and what cookies actually do Sign-in walls, session tokens, rate limits and DRM, explained without the drama. 6 min

How these are written

Each note starts from a question we were asked at the desk, and stays close to something you can check: a rule as written, a command you can run, an error you can reproduce. Where a topic is legal, we describe how the question is framed and stop short of advising — exceptions differ by country and by facts, and a website is not your lawyer.

Corrections are welcome and are noted in the page rather than quietly edited. Write to the desk.
