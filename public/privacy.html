Privacy

Privacy and cookies

Effective 19 November 2026. This page describes the whole of what the desk knows about you, which is very little and mostly by design.

In one paragraph

There are no accounts and no uploads. Your files never leave your machine. The site stores one preference on your device (your cookie choice) and, if you consent, Google may set advertising cookies. Server logs keep ordinary request data for 30 days. Nothing is sold.

Who is responsible

The operator of videoslocal.store, reachable at privacy@videoslocal.store, is the controller for the small amount of personal data described here.

What stays on your device

The desk is software you run locally. Links you resolve and files you download are handled by the engine on your own machine and are not transmitted to us. The only thing stored in your browser is:

NameTypePurposeRetention
vl.consent.v1localStorageRemembers whether you allowed advertising cookiesUntil cleared by you

Clearing site data in your browser removes it. The site continues to work.

Server logs

Like any web server, the host records the request line, the IP address, the user agent and a timestamp. These logs exist to keep the service running and to detect abuse, are not combined with any other data, and are deleted after 30 days.

Advertising and Google AdSense

Pages may carry advertising served by Google. When you choose “Accept all”, Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads, limit repetition, measure performance, and — where permitted — personalise advertising based on your prior visits to this and other sites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info and, in Europe, at youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Google's practices are described in the Google privacy and terms page.

If you choose “Essential only”, the advertising script is not loaded at all: no ad request is made and no advertising cookie is set. You can change your choice at any time using consent settings in the footer.

Legal bases

For visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: advertising cookies are used on the basis of your consent, which you may withdraw at any time. Server logs are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating and securing the service. For visitors in the United States, we honour opt-out requests as described below.

Analytics

None. There is no analytics script, no session recording, no fingerprinting and no third-party tag manager.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port personal data, to object to processing, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Because we hold almost nothing, most requests resolve to “we have no record of you”. Write to privacy@videoslocal.store and we will answer within 30 days.

California residents may request disclosure or deletion of personal information, and may opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly process their personal data.

International transfers

Advertising is served by Google, which may process data outside your country under its own safeguards. The desk itself processes nothing off your machine.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the effective date above changes and the difference is described here rather than quietly edited away.
