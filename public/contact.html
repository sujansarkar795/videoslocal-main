Before sending a takedown notice

This site does not host or index third-party video. If a video appears on a platform without your permission, the notice belongs with that platform — they control the copy. A notice sent here about a video we do not store cannot have any effect. The elements a valid notice must contain are listed in the terms.

Security reports

If you believe you have found a vulnerability in the desk, describe the steps to reproduce it and do not test it against anyone else's installation. We will confirm receipt and tell you what we intend to do, or explain why we think it is not a vulnerability.

Why there is no contact form

A form would need a database and a mail relay to exist, and both are attack surface for a site that otherwise stores nothing. Email does the same job with fewer moving parts.
